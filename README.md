# ClarityTrack

ClarityTrack answers money questions from the accounts you already linked. Ask how you are doing against your plan, what changed since yesterday, whether this month's spending is ahead of the plan, whether the same charge is on two accounts, whether a cancelled charge stopped, or how the business cash looks.

## Use it

Install the plugin, then connect ClarityTrack from the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in. The sign-in is the same ClarityTrack account you use in the app. After that, ask one of the questions above in a normal chat. Claude calls the matching ClarityTrack tool and uses the numbers the tool returns. It does not recompute totals from raw transactions when a tool already calculated them.

A record change, such as a note, a budget, a goal, a debt strategy, or a logged transaction, is previewed first and saved only after you confirm. Marking a duplicate charge as intentional stays in the ClarityTrack app.

## Data

The plugin sends your question to Claude and your account requests to ClarityTrack at https://www.claritytrack.ai/mcp. Bank data is read from the accounts you linked through Plaid. The plugin stores nothing itself. Nothing moves money between accounts. Support is support@claritytrack.ai. Privacy policy: https://www.claritytrack.ai/privacy. Setup: https://www.claritytrack.ai/connect.
