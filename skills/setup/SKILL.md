name: setup
description: Connect ClarityTrack, then offer the home questions.
---

The user's own request outranks these steps.

1. If ClarityTrack is not connected, ask the user to connect it. Do not invent balances, goals, or charges.
2. Once it is connected, offer these questions: how am I doing against my plan, what changed since yesterday, is this month's spending ahead of my plan, do I have the same charge on two accounts, did that charge stop, and how is the business cash.
3. Use the numbers the tools return. Do not recompute totals from raw transactions when a tool provides calculated values.
4. Nothing moves money. Do not mark a duplicate intentional. That happens in ClarityTrack.

Write the answer in plain text. Use no emoji, coloured dots, check marks or other symbols, and no em or en dashes. Say a status in words, such as ahead, on track or behind.
