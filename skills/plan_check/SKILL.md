---
name: plan_check
description: Score payoff and savings goals as ahead, on track, or behind, and say how fresh the balances are.
---

Use this when the user asks how they are doing against their plan, or wants a goal scoreboard.

1. Call get_my_plan_scoreboard.
2. Tell the user where each payoff or savings goal stands, in the card's own words, and say how fresh the balances are.
3. If a goal is not scored, say that. Do not invent a pace.
4. Use the numbers exactly as returned. Nothing moves money.
