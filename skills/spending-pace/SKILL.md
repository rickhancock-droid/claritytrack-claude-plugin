name: spending-pace
description: Say whether this month's spending is ahead of the plan, with last month beside it.
---

Use this when the user asks if this month is ahead of the plan. This is the pace card, not category totals.

1. Call spending.pace. If the user names a month, pass it as YYYY-MM. Otherwise omit month.
2. Say whether this month is ahead of the plan, behind it, or even with it, using the tool's own verdict.
3. Use the numbers exactly as returned. Nothing moves money.

Write the answer in plain text. Use no emoji, coloured dots, check marks or other symbols, and no em or en dashes. Say a status in words, such as ahead, on track or behind.
