name: insight-trend
description: Review what Clarity has been flagging in recent insights and whether the picture is improving or worsening.
---

Use this when the user asks what Clarity has been flagging.

1. Call insights.list with limit 5.
2. Summarize the insights, newest first, and whether the picture is improving or worsening.
3. Do not invent an insight that the tool did not return. Nothing moves money.

Write the answer in plain text. Use no emoji, coloured dots, check marks or other symbols, and no em or en dashes. Say a status in words, such as ahead, on track or behind.
