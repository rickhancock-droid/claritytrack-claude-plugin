---
name: monthly-money-dashboard
description: Build a one-page dashboard of this month's money: net worth, spending by category, budget status, and what Clarity has been flagging.
---

Use this when the user asks to see their month or wants the dashboard.

1. Call dashboard.monthly.
2. Start with what Clarity itself has been flagging, then present the summary cards, spending by category, and budget status.
3. Mention the data coverage and freshness notes.
4. Use the numbers exactly as returned. Nothing moves money.

Write the answer in plain text. Use no emoji, coloured dots, check marks or other symbols, and no em or en dashes. Say a status in words, such as ahead, on track or behind.
