name: money_story
description: Tell a short plain-English story of this month: what happened, what changed, and the one useful next step.
---

Use this when the user asks for this month's money story.

1. Call get_my_monthly_dashboard.
2. For the day-to-day change in connected balances, call get_what_changed.
3. Write a short story under 250 words: what happened, how spending sits against budgets, what Clarity has been flagging, and the single most useful next step.
4. Use the numbers exactly as returned. Do not invent a change. Nothing moves money.
